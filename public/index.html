How the index works

  • The basket holds every item averaging at least 10 trades and 25m gp a day (75m gp for equipment) on the GE over 30 days. Cosmetics, duplicates, notes, teleports, keys and lottery items are left out.
  • Weights. Each of the 13 categories has a fixed share of the index. Within a category, items are weighted by the square root of their daily gp traded, so busy items count for more but a few billion-gp items can't drown out everything else.
  • New items join after 60 days on the GE, once their guide price has settled. Before release they're simply left out and the other weights are rescaled.
  • Anomalies. A price that moves more than 10× within one comparison is set aside as a likely data glitch and listed under "excluded".
  • Data are official GE guide prices. The index is rebuilt daily, and the last update is shown at the bottom of the page.
